## Privacy

I keep this simple and collect as little as possible.

- **Comments:** when you comment, I store your name, your comment, and your email if you choose to give one. Your email is never shown publicly; I only use it if I need to reply to you. To protect against spam, the form uses Cloudflare Turnstile, and I keep a scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address, not the address itself.
- **Visitor stats:** I use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies and without tracking you across other sites.
- **Your browser:** the Mini Exam Simulator and the sound toggle save your progress and settings in your own browser. That information stays on your device.
- **I never sell or share** your information, and there are no ads on this site.

Want a comment of yours removed? Leave a reply saying which one, and I'll take care of it.
