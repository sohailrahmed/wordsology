### Antibody characteristics

The note about Le is VERY high yield. In fact, the entire card describing antibody characteristics is important. Undoubtedly, you will have at least one question based on characteristics of antibodies.

<div class="le-note">
<p class="le-title">The Le (Lewis) system</p>
<p>Lewis antigens aren't produced by the red cell. They're <u>adsorbed</u> onto it from the plasma.</p>
<table>
<tbody>
<tr><td>If the <b>Le</b> gene is inherited</td><td>the person has Le<sup>a</sup> adsorbed onto RBCs</td><td class="ph">Le(a+b−)</td></tr>
<tr><td>If the <b>Le</b> <u>and</u> <b>Se</b> genes are inherited</td><td>the person has Le<sup>b</sup> adsorbed onto RBCs</td><td class="ph">Le(a−b+)</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>
