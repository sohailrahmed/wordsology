Benton Station is a nice little slice of a town - less than 300 people - located in California, just west of the Nevada border. Seven miles west of Benton on a fun, undulating portion of California 120 - a mostly deserted road with unobstructed views all around - you'll come upon two towering structures. These bouldered mountains stand out starkly against the flat, brushy landscape. The journey up the top of the westernmost structure is my favorite place in the world.
